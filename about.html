WHO WE ARE

More Than Just a Photograph

Established in 2020, Kyra Picture is a photography brand based in Bekasi, Indonesia, available to travel wherever your story takes us.

The name Kyra is often associated with the Greek word for “lady.” For us, Kyra Picture carries a meaning of its own: warmth, connection, and the beauty of genuine moments. This is the feeling we hope to bring to every photograph we create.

THE KYRA APPROACH

Natural, Timeless, and Personal

We value genuine emotions, natural moments, and the little details that make every love story unique. Our approach is relaxed and personal, helping you feel comfortable being yourself in front of the camera.
