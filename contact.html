About Kyra Picture

01 — OUR STORY

Who We Are

Established in 2020, Kyra Picture is a photography brand based in Bekasi, Indonesia, specializing in wedding & prewedding, and engagement photography.

We focus on creating quality photographs with a clean, timeless, and considered visual style, capturing the important moments of every occasion.

02 — OUR APPROACH

Our Approach to Photography

Every event has its own character. Our approach combines attention to detail with an appreciation for natural moments and meaningful interactions.

We aim to deliver photographs that reflect each occasion clearly and can be enjoyed for years to come.

03 — OUR SERVICES

Photography Services

Kyra Picture provides photography services for wedding celebrations, prewedding sessions, and engagement events.
