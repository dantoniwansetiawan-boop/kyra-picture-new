PLEASE READ

Terms & Conditions

To ensure a smooth and enjoyable photography session, please review the following terms before making a booking.

  1. Session Duration

    Each package includes a photography session of up to 1 hour. The session begins at the agreed booking time. Late arrival may reduce the available shooting time.

  2. Participant Limits

    The Family package covers 1 graduate and their family. The Couple package covers 2 graduates and their family. The Group package is available for 3–5 graduates. Please inform us in advance if your group size changes.

  3. Unlimited Shots

    Unlimited shots means there is no fixed shutter-count limit during the booked session. It does not mean unlimited session time or unlimited edited photos.

  4. Edited Photos

    The number of edited photos follows the selected package. For the Group package, 30 edited photos are provided in total, not per graduate. Additional editing requests may incur extra charges.

  5. Original Files

    Original files are included and will be delivered digitally. Original files are unedited and may differ in appearance from the final edited photographs.

  6. Photo Delivery

    Photos will be delivered through Google Drive. The estimated delivery time will be communicated during booking. Please download and back up your files after delivery.

  7. Location & Additional Charges

    The package price does not automatically include additional locations, transportation, parking, entrance tickets, or other venue-related expenses. Any additional costs will be discussed before the session.

  8. Booking & Payment

    All bookings are subject to date and time availability. Please contact Kyra Picture to confirm your schedule, payment details, and booking confirmation.

  9. Rescheduling & Cancellation

    If you need to reschedule or cancel, please contact us as early as possible. Rescheduling arrangements and any applicable fees will be confirmed directly with Kyra Picture before your booking is finalized.

  10. Additional Requests

    Requests beyond the selected package, including extra shooting time, additional locations, or additional edited photos, may incur extra charges. Please confirm any additional requirements before the session.
