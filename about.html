OUR STORY

More than a photograph.

Established in 2020, Kyra Picture was built around a simple belief: the most meaningful photographs are the ones that make you feel something.

From the quiet moments to the joyful celebrations, we approach every story with care, intention, and an appreciation for the little details that make each experience unique.

Our approach is thoughtful, natural, and timeless. Rather than simply documenting how a moment looks, we aim to preserve the emotions and connections that make it matter.

Based in Bekasi, Indonesia, we are available to travel wherever your story takes us.

The Meaning Behind Kyra
“A little warmth can make a memory last forever.”

Inspired by the Sanskrit word Uṣmā (ऊष्मा), meaning warmth or heat, our philosophy reflects the warmth we hope to preserve in every photograph — the love, the connection, and the feeling behind each moment.
